## Special cases handled

- Wings (1): Won Outstanding Picture at the 1st ceremony. That night a second top prize, Unique and Artistic Picture, went to Sunrise; the Academy later dropped that award and counts Wings as the first Best Picture. Sunrise is not counted as a Best Picture nominee.
- La La Land (89): Announced as the winner by mistake after the wrong envelope was handed to the presenters; Moonlight was the actual winner.
- Moonlight (89): The real winner, announced minutes after La La Land was read out in error.
- 2nd ceremony (1928/29): no official nominees were announced; the five films are the de facto nominees the Academy later reconstructed.
- Category name changes come from the "Category name changes" list in the same Wikipedia article: Outstanding Picture (1927/28–1928/29); Outstanding Production (1929/30–1940); Outstanding Motion Picture (1941–1943); Best Motion Picture (1944–1961); Best Picture (1962–present).
- Split-year eligibility (1927/28 to 1932/33) is mapped to the later year; 2020/21 (COVID-extended) is mapped to 2020.
- CPI years averaged from fewer than 12 months: 2025 (11 months). Box office is adjusted to 2025 dollars.
- Language override: The Artist (2011) is silent; TMDB lists French (production country), so it is not counted as a non-English winner.
- Each film appears once; no film has been nominated twice. Remakes are distinct films and are matched by Wikidata item, not title.
